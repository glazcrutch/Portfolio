Personal Statement

I believe good AI starts with good data. My engineering philosophy is to build systems that are reproducible, observable and simple enough to maintain, because a model is only as valuable as the pipeline that keeps it fed and trusted.

I'm aiming for a career as a Data / ML Engineer, designing scalable platforms that turn messy real-world data into decisions people can act on. I'm eager to keep learning, collaborate across teams and contribute to impactful products.

“Make it work, make it right, make it measurable.”
