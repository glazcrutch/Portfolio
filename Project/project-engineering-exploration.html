Overview

This project was an introduction to how engineers actually work: define a problem, design a solution, build a prototype, test it and learn from what went wrong. It gave me a first taste of the full cycle rather than just one step of it.

The engineering process

  1. Understand the problem and its constraints.
  2. Brainstorm and sketch possible designs.
  3. Build a prototype.
  4. Test it and record what happened.
  5. Improve the design and reflect.

My contribution

  • Contributed to design decisions and prototyping
  • Helped test the prototype and note the results
  • Worked with teammates to divide tasks and solve problems
  • Reflected on what worked and what to change

Skills & tools

  • Prototyping
  • Design thinking
  • Testing
  • Teamwork
  • Documentation

What I learned

A first design rarely works perfectly, and testing is where the real learning happens. Clear communication within the team made the biggest difference to how smoothly the project went.
